## The open blocker: combining marks split words
The correctness and comprehension reviewers found this independently. The tokenizer (`src/wordfreq.py:7`) counts only letters and digits as word characters. Combining marks (accents and vowel signs stored as separate characters) count as separators, so:
- `हिन्दी` is counted as three fragments: `द`, `न`, `ह`.
- `İstanbul` splits into `i` and `stanbul`, because lowercasing `İ` produces a combining dot.
- `café` with a separately stored accent counts as `cafe`. STATE.md already lists this as deferred ("REQ-05 NFC-only").

I didn't fix it myself for two reasons:
- **It changes a requirement.** REQ-04 literally says "letters and digits", and marks aren't letters.
- **It is slower.** I built and timed a correct fix (marks allowed inside words, plus composing accents into single characters first). It makes the whole CLI about 1.7× slower: 1.4s → 2.5s on a 10 MB ASCII file, and 1.6s → 2.4s on a 10 MB non-ASCII file, at load ~5. SC-01 only just passed (3.79s, 6.42s and 3.84s on a loaded host), and a 1.7× slowdown would push those runs well over 5s.

**Options:**
1. **Defer (recommended).** Ship as is, add a REQ-04/05 note: "combining-mark scripts and decomposed accents are out of scope for v1", and add a `TODOS.md` line. The PR lists it as a known limitation.
2. **Fix for all input.** Accept about 1.7× slower runs; SC-01 would need re-timing.
3. **Fix with an ASCII fast path.** ASCII files keep today's speed, and only non-ASCII files take the slower tokenizer. This adds about 15 lines and two code paths.
